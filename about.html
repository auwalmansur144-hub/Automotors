Our Story

AutoMotors began with one simple idea: car buying in Kano should feel honest, calm, and personal. From our showroom on BUK Road in Kofar Kabuga, we've served families, professionals, and businesses across Kano State for years.

Every vehicle that joins our lot is hand-picked and thoroughly inspected. We only sell what we'd be happy to drive ourselves — sedans, SUVs, and 4x4s ready for the road.

Whether you're looking for your first family car or upgrading to something premium, we're here to help with patience, fair pricing, and after-sales support you can count on.
